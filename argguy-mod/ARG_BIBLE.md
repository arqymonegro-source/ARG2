# FRACTURED - ARG design notes

## Who/what it is (in the ARG)
Fractured is the entity from the YouTube ARG. It behaves like a hidden layer of the game: it is "seen" only when
unobserved, it comes with a wind chime, it floats through walls, and it takes people to a place that is not on the map.
In mod terms it is a fusion of wonderland.jar's **Spawn** (a person who broke and now crawls and laughs) and **Lithic**
(stone and bone that only moves unobserved) with Interloper's **Umbrella Men** (chime, groups, noclip, mutilation,
dragging you out of the map).

## Encounter loop
1. Stalk: grinding stone, it creeps closer only while unobserved. Look at it and it freezes.
2. Hunt: wind chime -> silent watchers appear far away -> whistle -> it floats through walls at you (model warps).
   Counterplay: keep eye contact (it freezes), but after ~8 s it shakes and breaks free; stand in water; trade stares.
3. Ambush (Spawn): if you ignore it for ~3 min it vanishes and reappears behind you, crouched, then charges.
4. Capture: `we got one.` -> Red Room dimension (3 connected rooms, silent CAPTURE copies) -> back after ~60 s.

## ARG hooks
- Codes/URLs: `tape.argguy.*.line5` (3 channels). The Demo Recorder's type text can carry puzzle clues.
- Cross-media audio: use the same wind chime + whistle in the videos so players recognise the hunt before they see it.

## Ideas for later
- Real "demo file" export; a static fake error screen after the ending; graffiti that returns after you remove it;
  more rooms/exits in the Red Room dimension (fake exits like wonderland.jar); a config file for stings/effects.

## Safety rules
- No strobing, rapid flicker or high-contrast flashing, ever (slow fades / vanilla Darkness only).
- Add a config to disable stings and screen effects; put a photosensitivity + horror warning on the mod page and videos.
